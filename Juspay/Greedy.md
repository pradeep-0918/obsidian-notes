# Juspay-Style Word Problems

### 1. Payment Gateway 💳

- Choose transactions to maximize cashback

**Pattern:** Greedy Sorting

---

### 2. Meeting Scheduler 📅

- Schedule maximum meetings

**Pattern:** Activity Selection

---

### 3. Delivery Fleet 🚚

- Minimum vehicles needed

**Pattern:** Interval Scheduling

---

### 4. ATM Cash Dispensing 🏧

- Minimum notes

**Pattern:** Greedy Choice

---

### 5. Fuel Stations ⛽

- Reach destination with minimum stops

**Pattern:** Heap + Greedy

---

### 6. Cloud Server Allocation ☁️

- Allocate servers efficiently

**Pattern:** Priority Queue + Greedy

---

### 7. Movie Streaming 🎬

- Maximum movies that can be watched

**Pattern:** Activity Selection

---

### 8. Airport Runway ✈️

- Minimum runways required

**Pattern:** Interval Overlap

---

### 9. Warehouse Loading 📦

- Maximize items in a truck

**Pattern:** Sorting + Greedy

---

### 10. Internet Bandwidth 🌐

- Assign channels with minimum conflicts

**Pattern:** Interval Scheduling
