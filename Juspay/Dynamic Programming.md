# Juspay-Style Word Problems

These are how DP appears in interviews.

### 1. Payment Gateway 💳

- Minimize transaction cost
- Cashback optimization
- Maximum profit

**Pattern:** Knapsack / DP

---

### 2. Delivery Company 🚚

- Minimum fuel
- Best route with constraints
- Maximum deliveries

**Pattern:** State DP

---

### 3. Subscription Service 📺

- Maximize revenue over months
- Skip months if needed

**Pattern:** House Robber

---

### 4. Game Level 🎮

- Minimum energy to finish
- Jump constraints

**Pattern:** Frog Jump

---

### 5. Investment Planning 📈

- Buy/Sell stocks
- Transaction limits
- Cooldown days

**Pattern:** Stock DP

---

### 6. Keyboard Typing ⌨️

- Minimum edits
- Correct spelling

**Pattern:** Edit Distance

---

### 7. DNA Analysis 🧬

- Similarity score
- Matching genes

**Pattern:** LCS

---

### 8. Warehouse Packing 📦

- Maximum value
- Weight limit

**Pattern:** Knapsack

---

### 9. Robot Navigation 🤖

- Count paths
- Minimum cost

**Pattern:** Grid DP

---

### 10. Exam Scheduling 📅

- Choose subjects
- Maximize marks
- Time limit

**Pattern:** 0/1 Knapsack
